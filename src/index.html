Variant Agentic Ladder · November 2026

From first prompt to being leading

Three levels for working with AI agents and AI usage for product development. Built from Variant consultants' own experience. At each level: what to train on for yourself, how to lift your team, and what to advise the client.

Pick your discipline, find your level, rate the topics, and get your own training plan.

The ladder is open source. Fork it or suggest a change on GitHub.

Use it from Claude or another AI assistant
  • Claude Code: install the coach with /plugin marketplace add varianter/agentic-ladder, then /plugin install agentic-ladder@agentic-ladder.
  • Claude.ai at Variant: the coach comes with the Variant plugin. Turn it on in your settings. With the Variant MCP connected, it can also compare the ladder with your CV if you say yes.
  • Claude.ai elsewhere: upload the agentic-ladder skill as a skill and turn on web fetch.

With the plugin, try:

  • "How does the agentic ladder work?"
  • "Where am I on the agentic ladder? I'm a designer."
  • "Make me a training plan, and read my CV first."
  • "Check in on my ladder plan."
  • "Help my team get better at AI."

Write in your own language. The coach answers in it.

  • Any other assistant: point it to llms.txt or the ladder for your discipline as Markdown, or use "Copy for AI" under your training plan.
  • When the assistant gives you a ratings link or JSON, open the link or paste it under "Import ratings". Nothing is saved before you confirm.
