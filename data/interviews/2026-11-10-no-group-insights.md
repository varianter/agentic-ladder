## About this file

Qualitative answers from agentic mapping in No group: what people had to learn to work well with AI agents, why it matters and how they found out. Interviews are short sessions an interviewer captured; self-submitted maps are the same sheet filled in by people on their own.

Each person’s answers are in the sheet’s order: what really good looks like to them, the tools they use, a 3 × 3 grid per dimension (level · question) and their reflections. Only answers someone gave are included.

People are numbered by when their sheet was made, and these numbers hold for this file only. Names, interviewers and private notes are left out; free text may still mention people or companies.

Edited for publication: client names, team sizes and remarks about identifiable people are removed or made general. Everything else is as exported.

**Dimensions**

- Individual: how you work on your own
- Team: how your team works together
- Organization: what your organization enables or gets in the way of

**Levels**

- Getting started: the first weeks
- Getting good: every day, at expert quality
- Next level: what you’re reaching for now

**Questions**

- What to learn
- Why it matters: what goes wrong without it
- How you found out: where the unknown showed up

**Reflections**

- What surprised you
- What you suspect you’re still missing
- Day one advice: first, and what can wait
